Help & Safety

Angle definition

Angles are measured from a square cut. A square cut is 0°.

Miter direction

Parallel miters have parallel end cut lines. Opposed miters face toward or away from each other.

Blunt edge

0 mm means a full-through miter. A positive blunt edge leaves a straight segment at that end.

Pointed and V-notch

Each shaped end uses two independent cuts. Enter both angles, or enter Cut 1 plus the junction position and let the app calculate Cut 2.

Workflow
  1. Select the geometry.
  2. Enter width, angles, blunt edges or junction positions.
  3. Check the live view and driven dimensions.
  4. Confirm hand, top/bottom face and scrap side.
  5. Inspect one first-off part before batch cutting.
